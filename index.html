Rings mark wavefronts, not literal audio cycles — spacing shows compression, not the true acoustic wavelength.
Classical (non-relativistic) Doppler shift for a moving source and stationary observer:
f_obs = f_source · c / (c + v_radial)
where v_radial is the rate of change of source–observer distance — negative while approaching, positive while receding. At closest approach v_radial crosses zero, which is why observed frequency drops sharply through f_source right as the source passes.
